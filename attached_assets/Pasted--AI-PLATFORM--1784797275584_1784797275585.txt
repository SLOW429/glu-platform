---------------------------------------------------
AI PLATFORM
---------------------------------------------------

GLU is not just an ebook application.

GLU is an AI-powered knowledge platform.

Artificial Intelligence should be deeply integrated into every part of the application.

Every AI feature should work independently while sharing a centralized AI service.

Support multiple AI providers:

- OpenAI
- Google Gemini
- Anthropic Claude
- Local LLMs (Optional)

The architecture must allow switching providers without changing the application.

---------------------------------------------------
AI BOOK CHAT (RAG)
---------------------------------------------------

Users can open any book and chat directly with it.

The AI should answer ONLY using information from the selected book.

Use Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG).

Features:

- Ask questions
- Explain concepts
- Explain chapters
- Explain paragraphs
- Explain difficult words
- Answer with citations
- Display page number
- Display chapter number
- Quote the original paragraph
- Show confidence score
- Related references
- Continue conversations

Example:

User:
Who killed the main character?

AI:
According to Chapter 14, Page 221...
